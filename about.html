About Our Church

Who We Are

A Spirit-filled Pentecostal community passionate about God's presence and power.

Our Story

Founded on Faith, Fueled by Fire

God's Gospel Ministries was born out of a passion to see lives transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit. What started as a small gathering in Bufumbanswa has grown into a vibrant community of believers dedicated to worship, prayer, and outreach.

We believe in the uncompromising Word of God, the power of prayer, and the gifts of the Holy Spirit. Our doors are open to everyone seeking a genuine encounter with God.

What We Believe

The Bible

The inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and practice.

The Holy Spirit

Baptism of the Holy Spirit with evidence of speaking in tongues and spiritual gifts.

Salvation

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, His death, burial, and resurrection.

Divine Healing

Divine healing is provided through the atonement of Jesus Christ.

The Church

The universal Church as the body of Christ and the local church as a community of believers.

The Great Commission

Fulfilling the Great Commission by making disciples of all nations.

Our Vision

Reaching the World with God's Power

We envision a community where every believer operates in their God-given gifts, where the lost are found, the broken are healed, and the next generation is raised up in the fear and knowledge of God.
